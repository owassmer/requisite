# 001 A filed contract round-trips (F1)

Draft from spec §15 beat 6, §9.3 and §12 F1, for Owen to confirm: two to five filed event contracts, drawn by a rule over
the filed record (one per strike type where they exist) plus the Western Grazers policy-relief specimen the spec names,
are parsed into the contract IR by an agent's proposal that code validates, their prose regenerated from the IR, and
both fuzzed. A user runs one command per contract and sees, in the static dossier, the IR, the regenerated rulebook
prose, whether the contract is supported exactly, with explicit assumptions, or unsupported, and the fuzzing report: the
feasible coverage set, fidelity, clarity and ambiguity, each clause linked to its IR node. Gate A (Mon 10:00 ET) is this
outcome's first pass: one F1 contract parsed to IR, the evaluator passing, one fuzz run end to end.

True when: the regenerated prose, read clause by clause beside the filed rulebook, means the same; for each settled
market of a contract, the IR evaluator applied to the settlement source's actual values gives the actual settled
outcome (spec §9.3: settled markets supply known outcomes); fidelity is measured by resolvers who never saw the IR; the
contracts were drawn by the rule, and the IR grammar was not changed to fit any of them.
