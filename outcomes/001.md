# 001 A filed contract round-trips (F1)

Draft from spec §15 beat 6 and §12 F1, for Owen to confirm: two to five filed event contracts, including the publicly
filed policy-relief contract (spec §9.3), are parsed into the contract IR, their prose regenerated from the IR, and both
fuzzed. A user runs one command per contract and sees, in the static dossier, the IR, the regenerated rulebook prose,
whether the contract is supported exactly, with explicit assumptions, or unsupported, and the fuzzing report: the
feasible coverage set, fidelity, clarity and ambiguity, each clause linked to its IR node. Gate A (Mon 10:00 ET) is this
outcome's first pass: one F1 contract parsed to IR, the evaluator passing, one fuzz run end to end.
