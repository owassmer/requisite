# U.S. Office of Personnel Management, Federal Holidays (holiday schedules by year)

## The observed-day rule of the us_federal calendar for a Monday-to-Friday week (5 U.S.C. §6103(b) for Saturday, Executive Order 11582 §3(a) for Sunday); OPM's published schedules are the cross-check for the calendar's tests.

> For these employees, when a holiday falls on a nonworkday -- Saturday or Sunday -- the holiday usually is observed on Monday (if the holiday falls on Sunday) or Friday (if the holiday falls on Saturday).
