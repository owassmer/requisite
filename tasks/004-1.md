# 004-1 Construction: from a deep case's exposure to the contract that best protects the business

## Why
Requisite's purpose is to protect a business from a harm it can see coming and cannot otherwise insure. A pending rule
would raise its labor or input costs, and its cash could run short before relief arrives. Construction (spec §8,
outcome 004, demo beat 3) turns the deep case's exposure into a new contract: the one that best covers the business's
cash shortfall within its budget. The contract is written in the contract language, so the one settlement function
(requisite/contract/evaluate.py) says what it pays in every scenario and when. The optimizer picks quantities to
minimize the liquidity shortfall's tail, and the report shows what stays uncovered and when the cash runs short.

The business is the one Owen promotes from the wide run today (outcome 002). The deep case (task 003-1, being built
beside you) gives its exposure register: dated monthly cash-flow deltas per pending event, as intervals, with the
event's probability. Build construction over the register's kind, never over one case. Test it on a deep-case store
built in your tests, so it runs on the live case the moment that case exists.

## What to build
In `requisite/construct/`, behind `uv run requisite construct <step> --case-dir <dir> --case <name>`:
1. **Observables (§8.1):** candidate observables for each exposure in the register's top k. An agent proposes each
   candidate (kind "proposition" or an observable proposal, whichever the definition holds; say which). Each candidate
   gets its source hierarchy, publication schedule and revision policy. Each is judged on the hedgeability questions
   (client_can_influence, source_authoritative, timely) through the recorded provider layer.
2. **Candidates (§8.2):** contract IRs generated by code from library structure templates, over the observables that
   pass. The families are digital, time-in-state (a strip paying per period an adverse state persists: for policy risk,
   since a digital pays the same whether relief lasts a month or years), and ladder or compound where an observable
   supports them. Variants cover retroactivity, partial relief and caps. Each candidate is a ContractIR the evaluator
   accepts.
3. **Payoff matrix and price (§8.3):** for each candidate and scenario, the payoff and its settlement date from
   `evaluate` (I8). The scenarios come from the register's event dates and probabilities. The price is a listed quote
   where one exists (requisite/market, read-only), otherwise indicative (judged probability × payout × (1 + loading)),
   labeled so, with the loading a stated parameter.
4. **Optimization (§8.3, A06):** the liquidity-CVaR program in requisite/optimize over the business's dated cash
   (liquidity profile, operating flows and the register's loss path). It reports the chosen quantities, total premium,
   the residual and breach probability with and without the hedge, the scenario table, uncovered scenarios and timing
   gaps, and each unbought candidate's reduced cost beside its re-solve's measured improvement. Shortfall is recomputed
   independently of the LP's slack.
5. **Robustness (§8.3):** re-solve across each forecast ± its uncertainty. Say whether the chosen structure holds. Where
   it flips, the responsible forecast is named as a high-priority question.
Parameters: the budget, the CVaR level α, k, the tolerance and the loading come from the case's DecisionSpec and
LiquidityProfile where the definition holds them. Where it does not, each is a parameter of the step, and its value is
recorded in the construction record and shown in the report. An interval-valued cash delta enters the scenarios at its
ends, never its midpoint (A04 §4.1). Optimality is stated only as the purpose phrases it: optimal within the declared
tolerance, under this evidence, candidate set, pricing policy and scenario model. Robustness varies one forecast at a
time, and the report says so. The construction report's dossier page is a later renderer (task 007-1's dossier); the
record must hold everything that page needs.
6. **The construction record:** a committed core record of all of it. If the definition has no type for it, write it in
   your report as the type you need, and the coordinator adds it.
