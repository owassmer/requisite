# 000-2 Wide-mode inference that matches its sampling design

## Why
Wide discovery (spec §7.2–7.4) reads the public record at scale: from the objective a human approves, the flow defines
its frame (for example, the public comments on pending rules that bear on it), reads items from it, and a decision model
labels each item it reads, cheaply and noisily. Owen then labels a random subsample by hand (gold). Decisions use the
aggregates: what share of the frame's businesses report exposure to which event family. A naive average of model labels
is biased whenever the model's errors differ across groups; the gold labels correct that bias. The correction is valid
only for the design that actually drew the samples, so the estimator has to be the one for that design.

The design the flow will draw is two-phase. Reading an item from regulations.gov costs one API request, and the key
allows 1,000 an hour (measured November 1), so a frame of any breadth cannot be read whole before the demo. Phase 1 is a
stratified simple random sample of the frame without replacement; the flow reads it and the model labels it. Phase 2 is
the gold sample: a stratified simple random subsample of phase 1, without replacement, whose strata may be formed from
phase-1 values (the model's own labels, so that rare classes are oversampled). Where a frame is small enough to read
whole, phase 1 is a census (inclusion probability 1) and the estimator is exactly addendum A07's finite-frame form. One
estimator therefore serves both. The demo's first beat (spec §15 beat 1) shows these prevalences with their intervals;
outcome 002 rests on them, and a wrong interval there is a false claim in the product's headline output.

  the estimator for a sample drawn from the frame.

## What to build
In `requisite/discovery/inference.py` (create `requisite/discovery/`): pure functions over arrays and a design given as
plain data.
- The difference estimator of a frame mean (a prevalence), for the two-phase stratified SRSWOR design above, and with it
  the design-based variance and its unbiased estimator. Phase 1 is a census as a special case, where the estimator,
  variance and standard error are exactly A07's finite-frame form (§8.2, §8.4: no Var(f)/N term). Model inputs are
  probabilities f in [0, 1], never top answers (spec §7.4).
- Power tuning (§8.3): fixed λ, and λ estimated without fitting on the gold sample it corrects (a pilot split or
  cross-fitting), with the choice and its assumptions reported. λ = 1, the plain difference estimator, is the one whose
  design-unbiasedness is exact, so it is the primary estimate; a tuned λ is reported beside it, with its measured
  coverage.
- The two-sample PPI++ mean, for a design the caller declares as two independent samples, and only there.
The design is described by the caller as plain data: frame size, the unit, strata at each phase with their frame and
sample sizes, and which phase-1 values define the phase-2 strata. It admits exactly these designs. The canonical
`SamplingDesign` record (addendum §12) is defined by the coordinator in `requisite/core/` and will map one-to-one onto
your arguments, so name them plainly and define no record that claims to be it.
