# 001-4 A filed contract read into the IR, and checked against how its markets settled

## Why
F1 (spec §9.3, §12, outcome 001) reads listed event contracts' filed terms into the contract IR. Settled markets supply
known outcomes, so the evaluator applied to an IR read from the filing can be checked against what actually settled:
the outside check that the IR means what the exchange settled. Gate A (Mon 10:00 ET) is "an F1 contract parsed to IR,
the evaluator passing, one fuzz run end to end". The parse is an agent's proposal (contract_reader), which code
validates and tests before it becomes committed state (spec I1; core.REQUIRED_GATES["contract"]: schema and tests).
A wrong IR must be caught by the gate, never fixed by hand.

## What to build
A Kalshi series is one set of filed terms listed as many markets that differ only in per-market fields (strike fields,
open, close and expiration times, the event). In `requisite/contract/harvest.py`, and behind
`uv run requisite contract read --case-dir <dir> --case <name> --series <ticker>`:
1. The series' filed PDFs (contract terms, product certification) extracted through requisite/evidence/extract.py, the
   extraction committed; the market fields read from the committed market records. Nothing re-fetched that is committed.
2. The contract_reader agent's proposal for the series as an instrument family: the support classification, its
   assumptions and unsupported terms, one representative market's IR, and the bindings, which say for each per-market
   listing field the IR depends on which IR value it fills (a node's threshold, a window's end, the timing's expiration)
   and which observable a market record's settlement value (expiration_value) reports, at which instant. Extend
   HarvestDraft in answers.py with the bindings, typed so that code can check them. The prompt is the coordinator's:
   write the text the prompt needs in your report, and the coordinator puts it in.
3. Code instantiates each harvested market's IR from the representative and the bindings, a pure function of the IR
   and the market record. It submits the series as a Proposal of kind "contract", whose payload is the
   core.HarvestedContract, the representative IR, the bindings and the market IRs (so each market IR re-derives from
   what is committed), through the store, so its gates run:
   - schema: every market IR validates as core.ContractIR and is accepted by the evaluator (no IRError);
   - tests: for each market settled by the draw's instant, the evaluator applied to the settlement value gives the
     market's actual result. The settlement value is an Observation of the observable the bindings name, from that
     observable's highest-precedence source, with artifact the market record (the exchange's statement of the source's
     value), published_at the settlement time, at the bound instant. The gate passes when every such market reproduces.
     A market whose IR has interpretive conditions or readings cannot be checked from the market record alone. Say so
     per market in the gate's detail; such a contract's gate passes only on the markets that can be checked, and names
     the rest. With no market checked, for any reason, it does not pass. The detail leads with the counts (checked,
     reproduced, not checkable) and states the check's scope (A09 §10.5): the value comes from the exchange's record, so
     it tests the IR's predicate, procedure and bindings, not its choice of observable, source or instant, which the
     clause-by-clause reading and the fuzzer test; and it says whether any checked market lay at or next to its
     comparison's boundary, since only such a market discriminates an off-by-one strike or an inclusive bound.
   - An unsupported series is submitted with no IR and its unsupported terms in the filing's words; its tests gate does
     not pass (nothing was tested), and the rejected proposal stands as the record of the finding.
4. The proposal, its gates and each market's result (reproduced, or the IR's outcome beside the actual one) are shown
   by `uv run requisite show`, and the HarvestedContract and IRs are committed when the gates pass.

## Done
Tests in `tests/contract/`, on recorded responses and test doubles of the agent (the test's own input, never a
cassette): the instantiation over a synthetic series of strike-ladder markets; the tests gate catching an IR whose
comparison is off by one strike, or inclusive where the terms say "above", by the market that settles differently; the
schema gate refusing an IR the evaluator cannot give one meaning to; the KXCAGOAT fixture's markets read into
proposals. Marked `@pytest.mark.covers("WP12.harvest_f1")` on the test that a harvested contract is classified
supported_exact, supported_with_explicit_assumptions or unsupported, never silently simplified. `gates` passes. The
live agent call and the live run over var/cases/f1 are the coordinator's, outside the sandbox.
