# 003-2 From the lead Owen picks to an open deep case on that business

## Why
Requisite finds a business exposed to a pending rule, then models its cash month by month and builds a contract that
pays when and as it is hurt (outcomes 002 → 003 → 004). The wide flow ends with ranked leads: businesses whose public
comments say a pending measure raises their costs. Owen promotes one at G1 (`requisite wide promote --rank N`, a `lead`
proposal, `requisite approve <id> --gate G1`). Nothing then opens the deep case. requisite/discovery/deep.py assumes a
case that already holds an approved entity model, an approved decision spec, the pending events as propositions, the
business's evidence and the deep templates' paraphrase sets. Its tests build all of that by hand
(tests/discovery/deep_case.py). In the running product the business is the one the flow found, so the case must be
opened from the lead, by code and the business's own words, with every choice a human's at G1.

What the manager sees when this is done: after the G1 pick, one command opens the deep case. It shows the business's
model (its revenue and cost lines, cash, credit and seasonality), each number quoted from its filing or stated as an
assumption with the reason. It shows the decision the case serves (horizon, loss, tail level, tolerance, liquidity
floor) and the pending measure as the event. The model and the decision each await Owen at G1. Once approved,
`requisite deep sensitivities | materiality | hedge | register | loop` run on that business unchanged.

## What to build
In a new module, requisite/discovery/lead.py, behind `uv run requisite deep open --case-dir <wide case dir> --case
<wide case> --lead <lead proposal id> ...`:
1. **The lead, approved.** Read the `lead` proposal, active by its G1 approval; refuse otherwise, saying which.
2. **The deep case**, its own store at `<wide case dir>/deep/<LeadPromotion.case>` named by LeadPromotion.case. Its
   evidence is the lead's own filing: the item, its artifacts, extractions and claims, and the committed ItemMeasure of
   the document it addresses with that statement's artifacts. Each is copied into the deep case as committed, under its
   own content-addressed id, so every number traces to what was retrieved. Nothing else is gathered here.
3. **The event**: the measure the lead's filing addresses (its ItemMeasure), as a core.Proposition whose window is the
   approved objective's horizon, proposed with deep.propose_event.
4. **The entity model**: the decomposer agent (its prompt as written; define its answer type in
   requisite/agents/answers.py) over the filing's claims and the objective's event class and loss. Code builds
   core.EntityModel from its answer, refusing a number that cites a claim the case does not hold, or that has neither a
   claim nor an assumption with its reason. It is proposed as `entity_model`, which awaits G1.
5. **The decision spec**, proposed as `decision_spec` (G1). Its horizon and loss come from the approved objective, its
   decision time from the frame's as_of and its entity from the lead. Its objective, alpha, tolerance, liquidity floor,
   actions, information and recourse are a human's choices: each is a required flag of the command, recorded as given,
   and the G1 approver sees them in the proposal. Never a default.
6. **The deep templates' paraphrase sets**, drafted in the deep case (wide.draft_paraphrases, each template deep.py
   reads).
The command prints the deep case's directory and the proposals awaiting G1, each with what it asks.

## What each record says
Every number of the entity model is either a claim of the business's filing or an assumption stating why no claim gives
it. The case holds only what was retrieved, by its retrieved id. The decision spec's parameters are what the human gave.
Where something cannot be opened (no measure committed for the item, a decomposer answer citing a claim the case does
not hold), the command refuses and names it. Nothing is filled in.

## Two things settled
- **Evidence.** Spec §7.1 names no gathering step (its step 2 matches event families to drivers). The lead's own
  filing is the business's evidence the flow found. A comment letter rarely states cash, credit or seasonality, so
  the model will be mostly assumptions, each with its reason. That is the honest model of what is known, and Owen sees
  every assumption at G1. The case's event is the one pending measure the filing addresses.
- **The live run is the coordinator's.** The decomposer and paraphrase-writer calls on Owen's pick are made live and
  recorded by the coordinator when Owen picks. Your done is on doubles, so a recording you cannot make is not part of
  it. If the decomposer's prompt cannot produce what core.EntityModel needs, report it, with the change you would make.
