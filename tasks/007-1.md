# 007-1 The static dossier: every case's committed state, rendered offline, byte-identical each time

## Why
A user sees what Requisite did in its static dossier (spec §13 WP16: "The dossier renders offline from a frozen case;
each clause links to its IR node, questions and evidence"; §8.7, the construction dossier). Every demo beat (spec §15)
is shown there, and the cold start (outcome 007) is true only when a fresh clone, replaying recorded responses,
renders dossiers byte-identical to the committed ones. Gate B (Mon 22:00 ET) asks for the static dossier with its diff
view; the diff (outcome 006: one change, one exact diff) is a later task that adds its page to this dossier, so build
the dossier so that a page for a committed record kind is one more renderer, not a rework. Nothing renders a dossier
yet.

The dossier states what the committed records say, and nothing else. It never asks a model, never fetches, and never
computes a number that a record does not hold. A number shown is a committed record's, with that record's id beside
it, so a reader can recompute it from what the record rests on. A status shown is its proposal's, with its gates and
their details. An unknown is shown with the reason its record gives.

## What to build
`uv run requisite dossier --case-dir <dir> --case <name>` writes a static HTML dossier of the case to
`<dir>/dossier/` (index.html and the pages it links), from the case's store alone, and also the dossiers of the
synthetic fuzz cases beside it (`<dir>/fuzz/*`), each marked synthetic wherever it is shown (spec I4). In
`requisite/dossier/`. Plain HTML and CSS, no script, no network asset.
- **Index:** the case, its records by kind with counts, and the review queue: proposals awaiting a human gate (G1–G4),
  each with what it asks.
- **Every proposal:** kind, author (an agent run, with its role and model), status, every gate with its detail, and
  its payload rendered for its kind. Rejected proposals are shown, marked, never hidden.
- **Filed contracts (F1):** each filed series with its filings (artifact ids, links to the extracted text), its
  support classification, assumptions and unsupported terms in the filing's words, the representative IR rendered
  readably (observables, sources, trigger as a tree, timing, procedure, payout), its bindings, and each market's
  settled-market check result.
- **Rulebook prose:** each clause proposal's sections in spec §8.5's order. Every IR node a section cites links to
  that node in the IR page (anchors by node id), and every IR node lists the sections that state it.
- **Fuzz reports:** coverage (obligations and their status, boundary points and their feasibility), each scenario
  with its split, severity, IR outcome and transfers, prose outcome, the resolvers' pooled distribution, its packet
  (the scenario proposal and its documents' text), fidelity, clarity and ambiguity against their thresholds, the hard
  gates, the equivalence claimed, and the attributions with their suspected clauses linked to the rulebook's
  sections.
- **Wide discovery (F4):** the objective and its G1 approval, the frame (searches, dockets kept and dropped with
  their reasons, item counts), the sample draws (strata, sizes, inclusion probabilities), the phase-1 labels by
  question and answer, the stability results where committed, the gold labels where committed. Each judgment is shown
  with its provider, model and cutoff, configuration and distribution.
- **Thresholds and other fixed values** the product judges against (the fuzz report's starting thresholds, spec §8.6)
  are not computed by the dossier: show them as the rule they are, citing where it is stated (the spec section, and
  the constant in the product that holds it).
- **Offline:** every page and every text it links to (an extraction, a packet document) is inside `<dir>/dossier/`,
  linked by relative paths; the dossier copies what it shows from the store.
- **Every page** states the case and the as-of instants its records carry. Identical stores render identical bytes:
  stable ordering, no wall-clock time, no random ids, no absolute paths.
