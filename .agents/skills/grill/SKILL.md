---
name: grill
description: Question the person until the purpose or an outcome is in their own words. Use before writing either.
---
Ask one question at a time. Frame it plainly, with what is at stake, then offer a few choices and
say which you lean toward and why. Who is the user. What can they do afterwards that they could not before.
What must stay as it is. What does done look like in the running product. Follow the words the
person reaches for and ask what they mean by them. Where earlier material already speaks to a
question, say how you read it and ask only where another reading would lead to different work.
Stop when the person can state it in one short paragraph without you. Write that paragraph in their
words and nothing else.
